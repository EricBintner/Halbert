## 3. Nominative Fair Use Statement

Halbert's use of third-party trademarks satisfies the standard tests for
nominative fair use:

1. **The product or service in question is not readily identifiable without use
   of the trademark.** Halbert cannot describe how to manage a `systemd` unit
   without using the word `systemd`.
2. **Only so much of the mark or marks is used as is reasonably necessary to
   identify the product or service.** Halbert uses the names in plain text; it
   does not reproduce third-party logos (except where a logo is part of a RAG
   source document preserved verbatim under its upstream license).
3. **Halbert does not suggest sponsorship or endorsement by the trademark
   holder.** This document and the footer of every marketing page state
   explicitly that Halbert is not affiliated with or endorsed by any
   third-party trademark holder.
