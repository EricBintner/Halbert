Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 · Last Updated: September 14, 2026

1. Core Commitment: Sovereign by Design

Halbert is built on an uncompromising principle: your computer's data belongs to you.

Unlike traditional cloud-based AI tools, Halbert operates 100% local-first by default. We do not harvest, monetize, train on, or transmit your logs, telemetry, configuration files, terminal commands, or conversational prompts.

2. The Halbert Software Application

2.1 What Halbert Accesses Locally

To function as a host custodian, Halbert reads local machine state on your system:

  • Hardware sensor telemetry (hwmon, CPU/GPU temperatures, memory and disk utilization).
  • Operating system logs (systemd-journald, syslog, /var/log).
  • Configuration files (/etc, dotfiles in $HOME, launchd plists, systemd service units).
  • Environment variables ($PATH, and Halbert's own override variables).

All of this data remains strictly on your local machine. It is stored in standard local directories: on Linux, per the XDG Base Directory Specification — ~/.config/halbert/, ~/.local/share/halbert/, and ~/.local/state/halbert/; on macOS — ~/Library/Application Support/Halbert/ and ~/Library/Caches/Halbert/.

2.2 Zero Telemetry, Analytics, or Crash Reporting

Halbert does not collect telemetry, event pings, or usage analytics. Halbert does not phone home on startup, execution, or shutdown. There are no background beacon scripts, tracking SDKs, or third-party diagnostic collectors embedded in Halbert.

2.3 Optional Cloud Model Connections

Halbert runs on local models (via Ollama or Apple Silicon MLX) by default. If you explicitly choose to configure external cloud API keys (e.g., Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or OpenRouter):

  • Only the specific prompt and relevant retrieved context snippets required to answer your query are sent to the designated cloud provider.
  • Your data transfer is governed strictly by your direct agreement with that cloud provider.
  • Halbert never proxies your cloud requests through intermediate servers.

3. The Halbert Project Website

3.1 Early Access Email Collection

On our website, we offer an early access signup form. We collect your email address solely to notify you when release builds or beta invitations become available. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your email address with third parties or data brokers. You may unsubscribe or request permanent deletion at any time by emailing hello@halbert.computer.

3.2 Hosting & CDN Server Logs

Our website is statically served via modern content delivery networks. Standard non-identifying request metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, referring URL, timestamp) is temporarily logged by CDN infrastructure for network security, DDoS mitigation, and reliable asset delivery.

3.3 Cookies & Tracking

The Halbert website does not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, Google Analytics, or behavioral cross-site trackers.

4. Commercial Purchases (LemonSqueezy)

For the paid tier (Halbert Pro for macOS), orders are processed by our Merchant of Record, LemonSqueezy (Lemon Squeezy LLC). Financial data (such as credit cards) is handled directly by LemonSqueezy and its payment processors and is never seen, stored, or processed by Halbert servers.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You possess the right to access or delete any personal data we hold (e.g. early access email list). Halbert does not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, email: hello@halbert.computer.
