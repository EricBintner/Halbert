### What Halbert has here today

Halbert's audio stack is real and local: four ingress kinds (`audio/ingress/local_mic.py`, `rtsp_ingress.py`, `webrtc_ingress.py`, `wyoming_ingress.py`) feed a fixed 16 kHz mono int16 PCM pipeline (`audio/pipeline.py:347`) through Silero VAD, a streaming sherpa-onnx zipformer ASR (`audio/speech/asr_engine.py:71-89`), speaker identification (`audio/speech/speaker_id.py:71`), an acoustic tagger (`audio/acoustic/audio_tagger.py:80-105`) and a sherpa-onnx OfflineTts VITS voice (`audio/speech/tts_engine.py:75-87`), with a `BargeInToken` (`audio/speech/barge_in.py:5-16`) that the synthesis generator polls. Voice turns reach the agent through the typed ingress lifted in packet 04 (speaker claims, modality, relay receipts, the D-6 RoleGate cap, the observe-only turn-event tee) and spoken replies pass through `integrations/tts_quality.py` (code-fence ratio, deterministic fallback line, `adapt_for_speech`), `integrations/speech_summarizer.py` via the utility slot, and the engine's `VoiceRiskPolicy` word budget (12/25/35 words, enforced cumulatively in the demuxer) — which, as the A10 refuter measured, is the real spoken-length ceiling and makes the summarizer unreachable. A second, screenless egress exists: `integrations/wyoming_agent.py` answers Home Assistant satellites. Vision is likewise further along than the discovery readers assumed: `vision/screen_capture.py` (MSS, downscale + JPEG), `vision/watcher.py` (periodic active-window monitor with a frame-hash change gate and OCR-on-change, dual-gated by `vision_config.yml` and the `being.yml` persona), `vision/redact.py` (OCR-and-blur of secret-shaped regions before any model sees the frame), `mcp/camera_gate.py:172` gating screenshot capture, and `tools/vision_tools.py` exposing capture with `max_dim` and region crops. What is thin: the wake word (`audio/speech/wake_word.py` hardcodes an ONNX backend for a `.tflite` model that was never trained, so VAD alone opens every turn), the wake-to-turn plumbing (no cooldown, debounce, dead-mic detection, duplicate-transcript suppression, or shared-room rule), the spoken-egress seam (two paths, one cleaner, no budget hint to the model, no playback-state truth), inbound media bounds (unbounded `images` and `audio_base64` request fields), and every audio model held resident for the life of the daemon.

Already lifted on these seams (do not re-propose): typed voice ingress + speaker claims + relay receipts (04-A1/A2, C2/C3/C4), the TTS quality rules and spoken summarization (04-C1/C2), RoleGate voice enforcement (D-6), the turn-event tee through the echo-guard seam (C4), the redaction variant registry, echo guard and camera gate (03, 05).

New in this pass versus the 2026-09-07 reviews: the entire wake-word family (Hermes `tools/wake_word.py` and `wake_word_engines.py` were unread), the sherpa-onnx open-vocabulary KWS, idle-unload of resident models, bring-your-own-CLI STT/TTS providers, the media limits table and bounded MIME sniff, the screen-activity logbook, the frame-token guard for coordinate actions, the output-activity tracker, the replayable event ledger, and the base-URL sanitizer. Extends a prior recommendation: the fast-context shortcut (OSS-REVIEW-OPENCLAW line 109 called it a direct lift; it was never packeted) and the exact-speech retained-set discipline (line 111, now pointed at a tracked Halbert defect). Called for in the packet-04 Hermes addendum and never built: duplicate-transcript suppression (A09-G2).
