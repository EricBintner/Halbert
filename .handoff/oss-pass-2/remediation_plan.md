## 1. Verdict

**Counts.** 221 gaps: **181 confirmed** (20 high, 70 medium, 91 low, after the refuter's severity adjustments) and **40 refuted** (all effectively low; 5 refuted at medium confidence and marked re-check in §5). 109 own-bugs: **105 confirmed**, **4 rejected**. Six units (A06 scheduler, A08 store, A10 tts, A11 lattice, A13 skills, A15 heartbeat) lost their full JSON; their gaps and bugs are reconstructed from the digest and the refuter's per-item verdicts and are as trustworthy as the others (the refuter re-verified every one of them at the real lines, and reproduced many by running code).

**Pure modules — solid, in places stronger than the origins.** Every audit says the same thing about the module that was actually ported as a file: the ranking shape in `continuity/promotion.py`, the three scheduler policy modules (`catchup.py`, `restart_budget.py`, `monitor_hash.py`) and the receipt store, the interrupt algebra core in `agents/steering.py`, the four-rank claim ladder and admission graph, the variant registry and echo-guard detection core, the verdict/gate contract, the lattice merge in `persona/permission/*`, the skills parser/catalog/truncation ladder, the utility-slot rung order, the MCP transports and egress choke point — all reproduce the origin's invariants line for line, and Halbert is *stronger* on a recurring set of axes: fail-closed defaults where the origin defaults open (claims UNVERIFIED, empty-intersection stub sets, missing-row deny in the lattice), no model names anywhere, no content stored in promotion signals, a DB constraint rather than a cursor for the one-open-leaf rule, defanged summaries at the prompt choke point, and a universal camera gate on MCP egress. The defects inside the pure modules are real but local: unclamped score components and a snapshot recency that never ages (A01-G2/G6), a corruption predicate that inverts the origin's rule (A08-G1), a column-0 fence regex (A10-G3), `Scope.from_mapping` rejecting the profile vocabulary it is fed (A11-G2), a `sorted()` that evicts the raw secret first (A03-G3), a gold answer handed to the answerer (A02-G1). Each is a one-file fix with a deterministic test.

**Wiring seams — not solid; this is where the program's value is still unrealised.** The origins' load-bearing durability and safety live in *where* the module is called, and that is what the ports skipped. Seventeen mechanisms were merged with no production consumer (§2 theme 1), so the docstring or design text claims an invariant the running daemon does not have: promotion signals are write-only, occurrence idempotency and `rebuild_fts()` have no callers, the ask axis is recorded and never enforced, `meets_floor` and `requires` are dead, the modality budget prompt block is unwired while the delivery side hard-truncates every voice reply at 12–35 words, the speech summarizer the C2 packet built is unreachable behind that cap. Where a seam *was* wired, the order is wrong at the one place the origin pins hardest: the mid-turn steer runs before the relay receipt and claim stamping (A09-G1/A07-G1, the single highest-consequence finding), the registry pass runs after the pattern pass (A03-G2), the catch-up run's completion lands on a sibling job record while registration blanks the parent (A06 own-bug 1/A15-G1), the FTS DDL runs before the degradation breadcrumb is read (A08-G2). Three more shapes recur across units: synchronous work on the event loop, fail-open on the exception path of an otherwise deny-by-default module, and tests/singletons that resolve the live data dir at import. None of these needs new research; every one has an origin test to mirror and a seam already named. The verdict is therefore: **keep the modules, re-wire the seams, and add the origin tests that were left behind** (A08 mirrors 1/20, A06 4/26, A10 2/20, A13 2/20, A15 6/20, A11 8/19).
