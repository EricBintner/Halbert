## A06-scheduler
SUMMARY: The three pure policy modules (catchup, restart_budget, monitor_hash) and the receipt store port their origin invariants faithfully — cadence-scaled grace, fast-forward-fire-once, one-shot retirement, bounded/staggered catch-up, sliding-window restart budget, receipt-before-side-effect, dead-owner interruption, closed status set, hash-persisted-before-run with a byte-exact diff cap (halbert-stronger), an explicitly named gated set (halbert-stronger), and a durable cross-boot restart ledger (halbert-stronger than OpenClaw's in-memory Map). The port is NOT as solid as the origins at the wiring seam, where the origins' load-bearing durability lives: (1) occurrence-level idempotency — the addendum's 'real at-most-once primitive' — is never wired (mark_started never receives a scheduled instant; catch-up never checks it), and combined with SchedulerEngine.add_job overwriting the per-job record at every registration and catch-up completions landing on a sibling '<id>:catchup' record, every reboot after the first inside a slot window re-serves the slot (duplicate morning reports on a day with two restarts); (2) boot recovery and boot catch-up are uncoordinated and use different APScheduler ids from the cron itself, so an interrupted recurring job can run twice concurrently at one boot — the origins explicitly mark an interrupted recurring run failed and skip catch-up; (3) the biggest functional hole is not lifted at all: APScheduler's 60s misfire grace drops any slot missed while the process is alive but the machine is asleep.
